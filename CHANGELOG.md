# Changelog

## 3.0-R1 (`AMTT-REL-001`), 2026-11-12

First public release. Changes relative to the pre-release draft that the companion manuscript cited at first submission are listed in Section 11 of the taxonomy document. In short:

- CRA strength labels re-derived from one stated rule. Every goal and leaf carries exactly one of four labels, with the condition under which it applies and what remains outside. Old and new labels are in `taxonomy-data/amtt-3.0/3.0-R1/annotations/strength-changes.csv`.
- Record-level rule added, including the limit of Annex I, Part I, point 2(f) to manipulation not authorised by the user.
- Stable ID, derivation type and source trace added to every node.
- ATT&CK mitigation descriptions moved to one catalogue. No method-to-mitigation assignment changed.
- Usage section with a worked threat record, limitations and citation metadata added.
- Machine-readable annotation files added.
